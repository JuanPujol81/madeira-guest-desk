# Validation framework

## Core hypothesis

Independent accommodation operators in Madeira will pay for a locally supported, human-approved workflow that reduces repetitive multilingual guest-response work without requiring a complex platform migration.

## Smallest disconfirming test

Present the €49 seven-day pilot to 20 qualified independent properties. The idea fails its initial commercial test if none pays after the offer, safeguards and deliverables are understood.

## Funnel events

Track these events without inventing missing data:

1. personalised introductions sent;
2. delivery failures and opt-outs;
3. replies;
4. qualified conversations;
5. examples or demonstrations requested;
6. payment-page visits, when privacy-compliant analytics exist;
7. paid pilots;
8. completed pilots;
9. customers continuing at €99/month.

## Initial thresholds

- Demand problem: at least 5 of 10 interviewees report three or more hours per month spent on repetitive messages.
- Assisted positioning: at least 60% prefer human-approved drafts to fully autonomous sending at the start.
- Willingness to pay: at least 1 of 20 qualified prospects buys the €49 pilot.
- Continuation: at least 40% of completed paid pilots choose the €99 monthly service.

These are decision thresholds, not current results.

## Experiment log format

For every experiment record:

- date and hypothesis;
- audience and selection method;
- exact message or offer;
- cost and time spent;
- sends, replies, calls and payments;
- qualitative objections;
- decision and next test.

## Current state

As of 6 September 2026, the site and payment link are live and ten personalised prospect messages have been sent. No replies, paid pilots, revenue, testimonials or measured time savings are claimed yet.
