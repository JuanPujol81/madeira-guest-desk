# Operations

## Customer journey

1. A property discovers the service through a personalised introduction or the website.
2. The operator confirms basic fit and schedules a 30-minute diagnostic.
3. The customer receives the written pilot scope and pays €49 once.
4. The operator collects only the minimum property information needed for the test.
5. Up to 25 approved answers are prepared in four languages.
6. Fictional or anonymised messages are processed in an isolated mailbox.
7. The customer reviews the workflow and results after seven days.
8. The €99 monthly service starts only after explicit customer confirmation.

## Pilot acceptance criteria

- Scope, price and exclusions are acknowledged in writing.
- No live inbox credentials are requested.
- Every draft can be traced to approved property information.
- A person retains final control over every response.
- Incorrect, uncertain and sensitive cases are escalated.
- Test data can be deleted at the end of the pilot.

## Delivery checklist

### Before the diagnostic

- Confirm the property and decision-maker.
- Record the customer’s main languages and communication channels.
- Ask for approximate weekly message volume and time per repetitive reply.
- Explain that the initial workflow uses fictional or anonymised messages.

### Before payment

- Send the exact pilot scope, price and cancellation terms.
- Confirm the invoicing identity and required receipt or invoice details.
- Confirm the start date and day-seven review date.

### During setup

- Build the approved question-and-answer list.
- Mark answers that require live availability, pricing or human judgement.
- Translate and review the initial response set.
- Test expected, ambiguous and unsafe message examples.

### Day-seven review

- Compare baseline and pilot handling time only when both were measured.
- Record draft acceptance, edit and escalation rates.
- Ask whether the customer would continue at €99/month.
- Document the decision without presenting it as a testimonial.

## Customer support

Primary channel: hello@madeiraguestdesk.pt.

Support requests involving payments, refunds, complaints, personal data or account access must be handled by the operator rather than an automated workflow.

## Outreach rules

- Contact relevant accommodation businesses individually.
- Personalise the reason for contact.
- Do not attach files to a first message.
- Do not use purchased contact lists, hidden tracking pixels or bulk BCC campaigns.
- Send at most one follow-up after an unanswered message.
- Record bounces, replies and opt-outs immediately.
