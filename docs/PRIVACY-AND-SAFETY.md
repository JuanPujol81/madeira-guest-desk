# Privacy and safety

This document describes the project’s operating safeguards. It is not legal advice and should be reviewed by a qualified Portuguese professional before processing real guest data at scale.

## Data-minimisation principles

- Use fictional messages during the initial demonstration.
- Prefer anonymised examples during the paid pilot.
- Collect only information required to produce approved response drafts.
- Do not place guest data, credentials or customer documents in source control.
- Define retention and deletion periods before using real messages.

## Human approval

The system may prepare drafts, but a person must review and decide what is sent. The following cases always require escalation:

- prices and availability without a current authorised source;
- refunds, disputes, chargebacks or cancellation exceptions;
- emergencies, safety concerns and medical matters;
- complaints, threats or legal claims;
- identity, payment-card or other sensitive personal information.

## Access controls

The founding pilot does not require a customer’s production inbox credentials. Any later integration should use the minimum permissions, individual accounts, multifactor authentication where available and documented credential removal at termination.

Passwords, one-time codes and recovery information must be entered only by the account holder and must never be included in project documentation or support messages.

## Required review before real-data operation

- Determine controller and processor roles.
- Sign appropriate written data-processing terms.
- Document subprocessors and international transfers.
- Maintain a processing-activity record where required.
- Define access, retention, deletion, breach and data-subject request procedures.
- Review email, booking-platform, AI-provider and payment-provider policies.

## Marketing safeguards

- No fake testimonials, customers, partnerships, credentials or revenue claims.
- No fabricated validation results or time-saving statistics.
- No dark patterns or automatic recurring payment without explicit consent.
- Outreach must identify the sender, explain relevance and provide a simple way to end contact.
